Visible Mending

Who says a repair has to be invisible? Visible mending turns holes, tears, and worn spots into opportunities to add color and personality to your favorite things. Instead of hiding the repair, you make it part of the design! These videos will show you some basic techniques to get started. Remember, there's no single right way to mend creatively—make it your own!

A little tip: Feel free to pause, rewind, and watch as many times as you need. Learning something new takes practice, and that's part of the fun!
