Embroidery

Think of embroidery as drawing or painting with thread! With just a handful of stitches, you can add flowers, patterns, and personality to almost anything made of fabric. These videos demonstrate the basic stitches and techniques to get you started. Don't worry about making every stitch perfect—a little personality is part of the charm!

A little tip: Feel free to pause, rewind, and watch as many times as you need. Learning something new takes practice, and that's part of the fun!
