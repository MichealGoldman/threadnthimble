Sashiko

Sashiko is a traditional Japanese stitching technique that uses simple running stitches to create beautiful, repeating patterns. Originally used to reinforce and repair fabric, it's also a wonderful way to add decorative details. These videos will introduce you to the basic techniques. Start with just one stitch, then another, and enjoy watching the pattern take shape!

A little tip: Feel free to pause, rewind, and watch as many times as you need. Learning something new takes practice, and that's part of the fun!
