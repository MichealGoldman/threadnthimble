Friendship Bracelets

A few strands of colorful thread, a simple series of knots, and you're on your way to making a friendship bracelet! These videos will help you learn the basic knots and techniques used to create different patterns. The trick is getting comfortable with the knots, so start slowly. Before long, your fingers will know just what to do!

A little tip: Feel free to pause, rewind, and watch as many times as you need. Learning something new takes practice, and that's part of the fun!
