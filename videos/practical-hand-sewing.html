Practical Hand Sewing

A little hand sewing know-how goes a long way! Whether you're replacing a button, fixing a busted seam, or hemming a pair of pants, a few basic stitches can help you tackle everyday repairs with confidence. These videos demonstrate the essential techniques, step by step. No sewing machine required — just a needle, some thread, and a little practice!

A little tip: Feel free to pause, rewind, and watch as many times as you need. Learning something new takes practice, and that's part of the fun!
