# Thread & Thimble — Website Brand Guide

Source of truth: the supplied Thread & Thimble visual brand sheet. Do not substitute an invented palette or logo.

## Identity
- Name: **THREAD & THIMBLE**
- Tagline: **Start small. Make something great.**
- Brand statement: “THREAD & THIMBLE is here to help you build confidence with your hands and enjoy the satisfaction of making something truly your own.”
- Personality: **Welcoming** (for all skill levels), **Practical** (clear, useful, helpful), **Creative** (encouraging and inspiring), **Timeless** (classic with a modern touch).
- Voice: warm, concise, encouraging, accessible to beginners. Avoid jargon and implying that visitors must already identify as experienced makers.

## Typography
- Display/headings: **Playfair Display Semibold** (600).
- Supporting copy, labels, navigation: **Montserrat Medium** (500).
- Tagline/decorative accents: **Caledonia Semi Italic**. This is a specified brand font but may require a properly licensed webfont. Do not silently replace it or load unlicensed files. If unavailable, use a serif italic fallback and flag the substitution for review.
- Use sentence case for most website headings; reserve all caps for small labels or the official logo artwork.

## Website design rules (recommended defaults)
- Overall feel: airy, approachable, classic-with-a-modern-touch; avoid busy patterns and overly playful effects.
- Content width: max 1100px; readable paragraphs max 68 characters per line.
- Spacing: 8px base rhythm; generous section spacing.
- Headings: Playfair; body: Montserrat; tagline: Caledonia italic when licensed.
- Keep text contrast accessible. The brand green on white may not meet normal-text contrast; use navy for small text, and green for large accents or non-text details unless contrast is verified.
- Buttons: rounded corners, clear focus states, accessible labels; primary navy with white text.
- Mobile-first layouts; ensure tutorial content works well on phones after scanning QR codes.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; avoid autoplay video.

## Site structure and content
- The public homepage is a quiet brand presence, not a marketing funnel. Keep it simple with brand introduction and contact email: **hello@threadnthimble.com** (mailto link).
- Do **not** add links to the tutorial/video pages from the homepage or navigation. Tutorial pages are standalone and accessed by direct URLs/QR codes included with kits.
- Do not imply online checkout or product catalog availability unless actually implemented.
- Preserve direct tutorial URLs when editing pages, since printed QR codes may point to them.
