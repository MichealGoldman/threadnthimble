Thread & Thimble

Thread & Thimble is a small, one-woman shop offering locally made gifts and hand sewing kits, as well as thoughtfully curated sewing supplies.

Whether you're learning something new, rediscovering an old hobby, or simply looking for a little creative inspiration, we believe making something with your own hands should be fun, approachable, and rewarding.
